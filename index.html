Governance & System Methodology

MealCycle AI adheres to strict Responsible AI guidelines (SDG 12), prioritizing human oversight, data privacy, and ethical decision support.

⚖️

Fairness & Equity

Models focus on aggregated daily student headcounts. Individual consumption profiling is explicitly prohibited to prevent bias.

🔍

Transparency

Every generated prep ticket displays explicit breakdown parameters — baseline model forecast, day weights, and applied safety buffer.

🛡️

Human-in-the-Loop

AI outputs are strictly recommendations. Authorized kitchen managers maintain sole authority over final prep quantities.

🔒

Data Privacy

Operates entirely on aggregated institutional attendance totals and anonymized meal counts. Zero personal identifiable information (PII) processed.

🥗

Food Safety Boundary

The AI system never independently certifies food as safe/unsafe for redistribution. Safety decisions strictly follow retrieved institution protocols.

🌱

Sustainability Target

Aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goal 12.3: Halving global food waste at retail and consumer levels by 2030.

Technical Model Specifications & IBM Technology Architecture

IBM BOB & SkillsBuild Stack

Prediction Model Architecture

  • Algorithm: Multiple Linear Regression (scikit-learn trained)
  • Training Dataset: 180 Operational Canteen Days (144 Train / 36 Test)
  • Model Performance: MAE ≈ 25.04 portions | RMSE ≈ 28.91 | R² ≈ 0.864
  • Model Features: Student headcount, Day of week (one-hot), Menu category, Event flag, Exam period flag, Holiday flag.

IBM watsonx & Granite 3.0 Integration

  • LLM Foundational Model: IBM Granite 3.0 8B Instruct (via IBM watsonx.ai runtime).
  • IBM BOB Framework: Deployed via IBM Cloud SkillsBuild API endpoints with client offline fallback.

IBM watsonx RAG Policy Lookup

  • Methodology: Keyword vector overlap & TF-IDF scoring over verified institutional policy text.
  • Safety Guardrail: Constrained prompting prevents hallucination or unauthorized food safety claims.

Entity Extraction Prompt Design

  • LLM Task: Natural Language parser extracting structured JSON entities from informal kitchen logs.
  • Human Verification: Extracted fields auto-populate the form but require user review before ticket generation.
